World

A world is the stack and the definitions (names) of one tab. Root, locals, the screen and the machine aren't part of it.

Each successful line produces a new world, and the last 64 are kept for back. A failing line leaves the world untouched (transactions). How worlds work explains the mechanism.

An installed disk saves each world. Tabs share only root, so definitions don't leak between them.

Inspecting it

words lists the definitions and depth list . prints the stack. stats reports the object count, collections, definitions, stack depth and history length.
