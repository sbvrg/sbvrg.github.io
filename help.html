Help

help is the manual inside Subverge. Each word has an entry with its stack effect, a short description and an example.

help                every word, by group
'math help          one group
'map help           one word

Without the quote mark, map runs instead of being passed to help.

The groups

syntax, stack, math, logic, flow, lists, text, io, names, world, infinity, memory, ports, time, screen, machine, dev, tools, demos, games, util and helpers. See words for the everyday ones and all words for all of them.

Your own words

Your own words have no entries, and 'foo help says so. Document them with # comments.

Other help

learn is the guided tutorial and apps lists the programs. see prints a word's definition (names).
